### 6. What did AI suggest you while building this that you deliberately rejected and why?
*Copy & paste this exact answer into the form:*

```text
While designing and building this project, I deliberately rejected three major AI suggestions:

1. Rejected Paid Ad Campaigns (Meta/Google Ads): AI initially proposed allocating the ₹2,000 budget to paid social ads. With an average B2B/EdTech cost-per-lead of ₹35–₹50, the ₹2,000 budget would have completely run out after just 40–50 registrations, failing the 500-student goal. Instead, I designed a zero-cost organic distribution model combining college WhatsApp groups, campus ambassadors (₹1,200 total stipend pool), and a post-registration referral loop (effective blended CAC of ₹2.40).

2. Rejected Monetary Cash Referral Bounties (₹50/referral): AI suggested incentivizing sharing with direct cash payouts. On a ₹2,000 budget, this is mathematically impossible for 500 students and actively invites disposable fake email registrations. Instead, I implemented non-monetary academic recognition badges ("AI Builder", "Growth Champion", "Campus Catalyst") and placement portfolio framing, which final-year engineering students value much more than micro-payouts.

3. Rejected Heavy Frontend Frameworks & Chatbot Popups: AI suggested using Next.js with an interactive AI chatbot popup on the landing page. I rejected both: the chatbot distracted students from the primary CTA, and heavy frameworks added unnecessary build friction. Instead, I built a lightweight, lightning-fast Vanilla JS/CSS + PHP PDO application compatible with standard WAMP environments and mobile WhatsApp in-app browsers.
```
